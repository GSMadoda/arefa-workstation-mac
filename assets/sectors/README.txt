Sector imagery for the Overview page.

Drop image files here with these exact names and they appear automatically
in the "Sectors in the cohort" band (any that are missing show a branded
icon tile instead — nothing breaks):

  fuel.jpg      Energy & fuel
  catering.jpg  Catering & events
  garden.jpg    Agriculture & fresh produce
  welding.jpg   Fabrication & welding
  cottage.jpg   Property & rentals
  spaza.jpg     Retail & trade
  arts.jpg      Arts & crafts

Notes
- .jpg is expected. Landscape works best (roughly 16:10); ~1400px wide is plenty.
- These are framed on the site as ILLUSTRATIVE sector scenes, not as photographs
  of a specific named enterprise. Keep it that way unless a picture is a genuine,
  cleared photo of that business.
- After adding files to this folder, copy the same files into docs/assets/sectors/
  as well (the site is served from both the repo root and docs/).

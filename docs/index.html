AREFA / VID3

Full bidirectional convergence, in eight techniques

The centre runs process mining over its own intake and support records. Forward mining traces each enterprise from intake to market; backward mining reads the market and the programme and folds it back into support. The two converge. In plain terms, eight techniques do the work:

  1. Discovery. Map how each enterprise moves through intake, compliance, mentorship and support — from the record, not memory.
  2. Conformance. Check that support delivery meets the programme's standards and milestones.
  3. Enhancement. Refine support structures from mentoring and resource histories.
  4. Performance. Find the bottlenecks — funding delays, capital shortages, infrastructure gaps — as numbers.
  5. Bidirectional convergence. Align each enterprise's operational challenge with the programme's objectives.
  6. Prediction. Flag enterprises at risk of stalling, so the centre can act early.
  7. Social network. Map the supply-chain links and B2B partnerships across the cohort.
  8. Automated improvement. Automate intake, verification and reporting with no manual friction.
About this build. AREFA here runs a Qwen2.5 model in your browser — the same model family the centre's system uses, adapted to the web. It is not the audited AREFA source, and reproduces none of its fingerprints. Nothing you type leaves your device.
What stays private. Each owner's phone and email, and each enterprise's stated weakness, were submitted to the centre in confidence — not published here. Barriers appear in aggregate only, so the case can be made without naming who is short of what.

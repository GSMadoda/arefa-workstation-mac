How the pipeline is tracked

The centre runs process mining over its own intake and support records. Stripped of the terminology, it does four things a paper file cannot:

  • Maps what actually happens, from timestamps rather than memory.
  • Finds where enterprises get stuck — a funding delay shows up as a number.
  • Reads both directions: what the market reports, against what the programme requires.
  • Matches the cohort to itself — the one with ICT stock and the one that needs a website.
About this build. AREFA here runs a Qwen2.5 model in your browser — the same model family the centre's system uses, adapted to the web. It is not the audited AREFA source, and reproduces none of its fingerprints. Nothing you type leaves your device.
What stays private. Each owner's phone and email, and each enterprise's stated weakness, were submitted to the centre in confidence — not published here. Barriers appear in aggregate only, so the case can be made without naming who is short of what.
